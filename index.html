Squish Shelf is a cozy squishy-toy game for when you need a breather.

Press and hold to squeeze: the longer you hold, the harder you press. Mochi sinks and slowly rises back, jelly wobbles, bubble wrap pops, caramel cracks and slime stretches between your fingers. Every material has its own feel, sound and vibration.

Hundreds of squishy toys

Mochi cats, jelly bears, crunchy snacks, soap cubes, pop-it boards, stress balls and slime. Every toy has a lifespan: squeeze it until it breaks, and the pieces stay on the table.

Blind boxes

One surprise at a time. Collect themed series and hunt for each season's rare and hidden toys.

Run your toy shop

Put your favourites on the shelves, welcome visitors and collect tips. Expand to a showroom, an upstairs floor, a sunny terrace and a secret vault. Rare toys you wear out become specimens for your display cabinet.

Questions

What is Squish Shelf?

A relaxing squishy-toy game for phones and browsers. You press, stretch and poke realistic squishy toys, open blind boxes to collect series, and run a small toy shop.

Is Squish Shelf free?

Yes. It is free to play with optional in-app purchases (gems, a monthly club, a season pass). Every day you get a free taster toy, free restocks and enough tickets to open about one blind box a day without paying.

Does it have ads?

No. Squish Shelf has no ads.

Where can I play it?

On Android through Google Play, on itch.io, and directly in your browser at squish.vitacazap.com/play.

Which languages does it support?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Squeezing toys works offline; the leaderboard and friend invites need a connection.

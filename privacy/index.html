Squish Shelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Squish Shelf ("the game") is published by DESCU AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game has no ads, you do not need an account to play, and your progress stays on your device. We use Google Analytics to learn how the game is played, without advertising features.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (toys, coins, gems, settings) is saved only on your phone. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Weekly leaderboards

When you open a leaderboard, the game sends a random player id (created on your device, not linked to you), the nickname and avatar you picked in the game, and this week's scores (presses, perfect presses, toys collected). Your nickname, avatar and scores are shown to other players on that week's board. Do not use your real name as a nickname if you do not want it shown. Scores are deleted after 8 weeks.

Friend invites

Each install gets an invite code. To count how many days you have played (so invite rewards go to real players), the game sends your random player id once a day. When you enter a friend's code, we store which code you entered and a one-way hash of your IP address, used only to stop the same network from claiming many rewards. We do not store the IP address itself.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture card on your device and hands it to your phone's or browser's share menu. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Analytics

The game and this website use Google Analytics to count things like how many people play each day, which screens they use, how many boxes are opened and which purchases are made (product and price only). Google Analytics sets a cookie or similar identifier and receives your IP address, which Google uses to estimate your country and then discards. Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, and we do not link analytics to your purchases or to any name. You can see how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In browsers you can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or a content blocker.

Purchases

The game is sold through several stores. Each store handles its own payments, and we never see your card number or bank details.

  • Google Play (Android). When you buy something, the game sends the product id and the purchase token that Google Play issues to our server, so we can confirm with Google that the purchase is real before delivering it. We keep the token, the product id, the Google order id and the purchase status.
  • itch.io. You pay on our itch.io page. To deliver what you paid for, the game asks you to sign in with itch.io; we receive only your itch.io user id (not your password or email), look up your payments for this game with itch.io, and keep the payment id, amount, date and your itch.io user id.
  • Website (card payments). Card payments on our website are processed by Stripe. We keep the Stripe checkout id, the product, the amount and the payment status.

We use these records only to deliver purchases, prevent fraud and help if you contact us about a purchase.

What we do not collect

  • No real name, email or phone number. Signing in is only needed to receive an itch.io purchase.
  • No advertising id and no ads.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising.

Service providers

Purchase records, leaderboard scores and invite records are stored with Supabase (our database host). Analytics is provided by Google Analytics. Purchases are checked with the store that took the payment: Google Play (Google Privacy Policy), itch.io (itch.io Privacy Policy) or Stripe (Stripe Privacy Policy).

Retention and deletion

Purchase records are kept for as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and legal accounting requirements, and at most 5 years. Invite records are kept while the invite program runs, and at most 2 years. Google Analytics data is kept for 2 months. To ask us to delete your leaderboard or invite records, email us your invite code (shown on your profile). To ask us to delete the purchase records tied to an order, email us with your order id (Google Play order ids start with GPA.; for itch.io or card payments, send the date and amount).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

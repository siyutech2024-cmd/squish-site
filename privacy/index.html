Squish Shelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Squish Shelf ("the game") is published by DESCU AI ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (toys, coins, gems, settings) is saved only on your phone. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details, name or email address. When you buy something, the game sends the product id and the purchase token that Google Play issues for that purchase to our server, so we can confirm with Google that the purchase is real before delivering it. We keep the token, the product id, the Google order id and the purchase status to prevent fraud and to help if you contact us about a purchase. We do not link this to your identity.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising id and no ads.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Service providers

Purchase records are stored with Supabase (our database host) and checked against Google Play. Google's own handling of payments is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Purchase records are kept for as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and legal accounting requirements, and at most 5 years. To ask us to delete the purchase records tied to an order, email us with your Google Play order id (it starts with GPA.).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
